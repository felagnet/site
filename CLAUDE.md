# felag-site

One static page for felag.net, generated by Python and deployed by GitHub Pages on every push to main.

## Where things live
- Copy, CSS and motion: felagsite.py, content constants at the top. Page chrome and tokens: shared.py. Hero canvas: hero8-rune.js.
- out/ and dist/ are build output. Never edit them; edit the source and rebuild.
- The harvest page is a kept prototype. Do not touch hero6-field.js, lib/ or anything that builds only the harvest page.

## After every change
Run python3 build_site.py, python3 make_dist.py and python3 check.py. All checks pass before a commit. Show git diff --stat; only source files, DESIGN.md, PRODUCT.md and .impeccable/ may change. One commit per change.

## Copy is locked
Do not change any visible text, title, meta tag, alt text, aria label or link. When a command suggests a copy change, or finds missing alt text or labels, write it to .impeccable/review/copy-notes.md with the section and the reason, and leave the source as it is.

## Design
DESIGN.md is the master of the Felag design system. Read it before any visual change.
- Dark theme on the site. Copper is the only accent: the mark, links, the primary button and one emphasis per page.
- Schibsted Grotesk for everything, IBM Plex Mono for code and identifiers, nothing else. Sentence case, never all caps. Tabular lining figures on every number, price and metric.
- 4 px spacing scale from 4 to 64. 12 columns, 24 px gutter, 1200 px content width.
- Corners 0, 2 or 4 px, no pills. 1 px hairlines.
- Copper light is part of the system, as DESIGN.md defines it: ignition flashes, warm blooms on hover, the soft lift under stacked panels. Never on a body surface at rest, never a second colour.
- The mark is four copper pieces with three channels. Its geometry in the header, hero and closing scene stays exactly as it is. Never rotate it, recolour pieces, round corners or fill the channels. Its glow follows DESIGN.md.
- Never: runic alphabets as decoration, Norse imagery, hexagons, chains and nodes, circuit patterns, isometric cubes, stock photos, emoji, purple, neon green, a second accent colour.
- No em dashes and no exclamation marks anywhere, including comments and commit messages.

## Motion
- With prefers-reduced-motion the page shows the finished mark in the hero and the closing scene, with no animation.
- Safari has no canvas blur filter; hero8-rune.js carries a shadow-blur fallback. Keep it, and check Safari or WebKit after any motion change.

## Do not add
Analytics, cookie banners, chat widgets, third-party scripts, new fonts or new dependencies for the page.
The one exception is Plausible, the cookieless analytics Andrew chose on 22 October 2026: its snippet in the head of index.html and the Contact form sent event. No other analytics, no cookies, no consent banner.
